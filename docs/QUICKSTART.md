# Beginner guide

## Start a campaign

Choose **Start game** and allocate all five bonus points. Strength helps health and attack; Dexterity helps dodging and theft; Wisdom helps identification and wearing magical equipment; Eloquence helps negotiation and mercy; Luck contributes to theft rolls. Read or skip the illustrated prologue.

Your goal is to repay 20,000 gold and defeat your family's nemesis. A new game starts with 1,000 gold and no free stock.

## Buy and display goods

The travelling merchant visits every Monday. Click the wagon to browse. Drag quantity controls or use the one-unit arrow buttons, then select **Buy**. You can revisit the caravan during Monday between customers; its stock does not reset on re-entry.

**Warehouse** contains stored goods. Choose a shelf, then an item. Select **Bargain** or **Marked price**, choose quantity and price, and select **Place / replace**. Replaced stock returns to storage. **Restock shelf** moves more stored units onto the same display. Sold-out shelves become **Empty**.

Only displayed, stocked items attract customers. Stored goods are not automatically for sale. Shelves hold one item type each, up to twenty units.

## Make a sale

Select **Admit customer**. Their requested item appears on the counter. The journal book in the lower-left opens their history.

- **Marked price:** confirm or decline the sale.
- **Bargain:** the customer offers first. Drag your price marker, inspect the chance and patience indicators, then click the D20 to attempt persuasion. You can accept their offer instead.
- A higher demand can produce more profit but is harder to justify. Repeated refusals worsen mood and can end negotiation.
- After agreement, confirm the sale and click the money pouch. Payment is collected only once.
- Select **See them out** to finish the visit.

The cost and profit indicators are for you. Marked prices are limited to 15% above acquisition cost; bargaining can reach higher prices. A shopper's carried money and willingness to pay are different quantities.

## Manage your money and time

There are up to ten customers each day. The daily report shows revenue, not profit. **Skip day** closes early and advances no invented sales; use **Start next day** on the report.

Weekly rent is 200 gold. Monthly repayment is 1,000 gold. **Repay** lets you repay early. Keep enough gold for stock and equipment. If your business has no cash or recoverable assets left, open **Menu** and start a new campaign. The game archives the previous save automatically.

## Equipment and potions

Open **Character**, or select an owned item in **Warehouse**, to equip it. You have weapon, armour and two accessory slots. Requirements use Strength, Dexterity and/or Wisdom. White items are common, blue magical, yellow rare and orange legendary. Identification reveals hidden affixes; wearing depends on attribute requirements.

The merchant sells **Life Potions** for a base 20 gold each. They remain in storage until displayed or consumed. Click the potion icon in the upper toolbar to drink one and restore up to 40 HP. Full-health characters cannot drink one. During combat, drinking restarts your own attack timer. Enemies who have potions may drink at low health and restart their own timer too.

## Theft, wanted criminals and combat

**Belongings** lets you inspect a visitor's carried gear. You can buy equipment or attempt theft. Repeated theft attempts get harder. Being caught can provoke insults, intimidation or a confrontation. **Fight** begins combat; **Beg for mercy** attempts a personality-dependent D20 check.

A red **WANTED** mark identifies a criminal you may attack. Combat attacks resolve automatically when each fighter's bar fills. Armour reduces damage and adds block chance; Dexterity adds dodge chance. Equip gear and stock potions before starting a fight.

After a wanted victory, click the treasure chest: gold goes to your balance and equipment to storage. Select **Finish encounter** afterwards. At zero HP, the opponent may take one or two worn items. The defeat report lists them; **Rest** ends the day and restores health the next morning.

## Commissions and progression

Meet customers and see them out before hiring them through **Commissions**. Choose an available adventurer and destination. You may lend one compatible weapon and one armour item from storage; a regional relic provides another bonus. Check the cost, duration, success chance and loss risks before funding.

Advance days, reopen **Commissions**, then collect the returned report. Successful trips provide loot and can unlock the next region. Failed trips may return surviving loan equipment but award no success loot. Better regional gear, talents and equipped affixes help your character progress.

Experience from sales, commissions and victories earns talent points. Open **Talents** and choose a discipline. Some branches require an earlier talent.

After the debt is fully repaid, prepare for the final duel. Defeat the nemesis to see the ending.

## Save and exit

Successful actions save automatically. **Save** also writes your progress. Close the launcher window to stop the server. Reopen the game and select **Continue** to resume. The executable stores saves in `%LOCALAPPDATA%\DungeonMerchant`; source runs use the project's `saves` folder.
